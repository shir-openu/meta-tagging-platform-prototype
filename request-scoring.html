Refused before payment

  • A term with one settled definition. There is nothing to score.
  • A term this corpus and your papers barely use — a board needs attested uses, not a dictionary entry.
  • A request for us to decide which definition is right. The board reports how each one performs against adjudicated cases; it does not adjudicate the field.

What you get, and what you can check

Every case carries the exact sentence from the paper it was read from, so any claim the board makes can be checked against its source. The circular definition is there to check us: it copies the answer, so it must score near +1.000. If it does not, the coding contradicts itself and no other number in the table may be read.
