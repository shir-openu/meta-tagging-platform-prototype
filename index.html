What this is

A tag layer for academic literature. Every tag carries the exact sentence from the paper it was read from, so any claim it makes can be checked against the source.

What makes it different

Definitions are not asserted, they are scored. Rival definitions of a concept are ranked against adjudicated cases, and a deliberately circular definition is kept in the table as a control: if it wins, the ranking has measured nothing.

What is not finished

Three concepts have a scored board. The corpus holds far more terms that are defined more than one way, and those are shown without a score. Planned next: the negative space — terms the literature should have coined and did not.

The data is open

Every number here is reproducible from the data files; reference_scorer.py recomputes the scores from them. Also README, llms.txt and other projects.

The tag layer is CC BY 4.0. Paper text belongs to its authors and is linked, never reproduced.
